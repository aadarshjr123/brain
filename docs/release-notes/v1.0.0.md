# Brain 1.0

Brain is a free map of how frontend development touches the browser, for anyone relearning the model behind the
code: before an interview, during a performance fix, or out of curiosity.

## What is in it

- **152 concepts, 301 named relations**, eight layers, every concept with a source.
- **Explore**: click a concept to unfold what it drives and what drives it.
- **Route**: pick two concepts and watch the shortest chain of cause and effect, explained hop by hop.
  Try [setState to the GPU process](/explore?from=set_state&to=gpu_process).
- **Concept pages and a printable cheatsheet** that work without JavaScript.
- **Your progress stays yours**: kept in your browser, exportable, no account.
- **Works offline** after your first visit, in light and dark, with a keyboard and with a screen reader.

## Known limits

- Content is English only.
- Concept pages have a summary and one source each; longer explanations come later.
- Share images use a system font rather than the site's typeface.
- Progress does not sync between devices on its own; use Export and Import.

## Thanks

Facts come from the WHATWG and ECMAScript specs, the IETF RFCs, MDN, web.dev, the Chrome and V8 teams' writing and
react.dev. Found a mistake? Every concept page has a "Report it" link.
